The company

Steven Malog, Inc. is a technology company that builds and operates a small family of software products. We work quietly and ship real things — tools for events, data, discovery and the everyday running of a business.

Incorporated in Delaware as a C Corporation, with its working home in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. One studio, several products, and a name we intend to keep honest.
